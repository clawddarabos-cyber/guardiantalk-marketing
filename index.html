Questions, answered

Built for trust on both sides of the screen.

Can my child see that GuardianTalk is running?+

Yes. GuardianTalk is designed to be visible and understandable. Children can see when it is active and what kinds of information are included in family reports.

Does GuardianTalk record the screen or read every message?+

No. GuardianTalk is designed around activity patterns and categories—not secret recordings, keystrokes, passwords, or a feed of private messages.

What is included for free?+

The free plan provides one concise daily breakdown for one child computer. Interactive questions and deeper multi-day insights are part of GuardianTalk+.

Where is family data stored?+

GuardianTalk is being built local-first. Raw activity remains on family-controlled devices whenever possible, with encryption and clear deletion controls for any synced information.
